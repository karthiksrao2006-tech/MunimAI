From hackathon demo to district pilot

Weeks 1–2 · Field

Interview 15 owners, 3 accountants, 2 branch managers using the 12 field questions. Lock the persona.

Weeks 3–4 · Data

Synthetic ledgers on PS-BOSS. Scheme rulebook with clause IDs. GST sandbox wired up.

Weeks 5–6 · Agents

Orchestrator, six agents and the verifier on WhatsApp. Evaluation set of 300 cases.

Week 7 · Demo

End-to-end loop, fairness report, banker-view dashboard. Simulated filing.

Months 3–9 · Pilot

100 businesses with one SHG federation and one lender partner. Measure time saved, approvals.
