A month with MunimAI

From a bag of bills to a sanctioned loan

Day 1

Lakshmi forwards 40 bill photos. Ledger agent books them and flags 3 unclear ones.

Day 10

Compliance agent drafts GSTR-1, spots a supplier ITC mismatch and messages the supplier.

Day 11

Owner approves. Accountant files in minutes, not hours. Late fee avoided.

Day 14

Scheme agent: “You qualify for MUDRA Kishore and CGTMSE cover”, with the clauses.

Day 20

Consent via AA. Credit agent builds a score with reasons. Packet sent to 2 lenders.

Demo goal: show this whole loop live in 4 minutes on synthetic data, with filing and submission simulated.

This is the story we demo. Use one continuous synthetic business so the judges see data flow from bills to compliance to credit. Mention that the accountant also benefits: half the sorting arrives done, so they can carry more clients.
