The solution

One WhatsApp number. Four jobs done.

1 · Capture

Bills in, books out

Photo, voice note or forward. OCR plus an LLM builds the ledger in Kannada, Hindi or English.

2 · Comply (3.1)

GST and e-way, prepared

GSTR-1 and 3B drafts, ITC mismatch alerts, e-way bills before dispatch. Filing stays with the owner.

3 · Match

Schemes, with sources

Checks MUDRA, PMEGP, Stand-Up India, CGTMSE rules and cites the clause for every yes or no.

4 · Apply

A file a banker trusts

Consented UPI and bank data, an explainable cash-flow score, every document in one packet.

The owner approves every step. Nothing is filed or submitted on their behalf.

Walk across the four cards. Stress that the owner never has to install an app or fill a form. WhatsApp plus voice is the interface. Compliance (3.1) is card 2, and it feeds cards 3 and 4.
