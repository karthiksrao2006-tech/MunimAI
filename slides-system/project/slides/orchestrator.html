The orchestrator plans in days, not turns

Built on LangGraph. It is not a chatbot replying to one message — it holds a plan and a memory that spans a whole GST cycle.

Day 1

40 bill photos arrive. Orchestrator hands them to the Ledger agent, flags 3 unclear ones, waits.

Day 10

Deadline approaching. It wakes the Compliance agent on its own, drafts GSTR-1.

Day 10

Compliance agent finds an ITC mismatch. Orchestrator decides: message the supplier first, then ask the owner.

Day 20

Routes to Scheme + Credit agents only once GST is clean — it knows the order matters.

State it holds per business: ledger status, filing deadlines, open consent tokens, which agent is waiting on what, and a full audit trail of every decision — in Postgres, not in the model's context window.

This is the difference between "agentic" and "a chatbot with tools." The orchestrator decides when to act without being asked, across days, and remembers why it made each call.
