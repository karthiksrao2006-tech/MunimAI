1 · The mesh, not a cable

There is no office LAN between these two machines. Tailscale gives each device a stable 100.x.x.x address and punches a direct, encrypted path between them over the open internet — a coordination server only introduces the pair once.

2 · What changed today: the SSH gate

Tailscale SSH sits in front of the OS login and decides, per policy, whether a session needs a fresh human check. Before today it did; now it doesn't — this is the exact difference that unblocked the agent.
